## What's New (release notes)

A bigger sound library, and sessions that never have to end.

24 soundscapes, up from 15. Nature, Music, and Frequencies, now eight each, with
new textures like Babbling Brook, Campfire, Birdsong, Warm Strings, Kalimba,
Flute, White Noise, Green Noise, and Alpha.

All-new audio. Every soundscape was generated with ElevenLabs' latest music model

Instant previews. Tap any soundscape and it starts right away, with a gentle fade.

Every soundscape now has its own quiet piece of line art that moves with it in the
player.

As always, every session is written fresh for you and voiced on the spot, and it
keeps playing when your screen locks.

## Description (full)

relaxed makes a meditation just for you. Tell it how you feel or what you need,
choose how long you have, and it writes a session for that moment and voices it on
the spot, over a continuous soundscape. No two are quite the same.

New in this version:

- 24 seamless soundscapes across Nature, Music, and Frequencies, generated with
  ElevenLabs' latest music model and finished by a producer, including rain, ocean,
  thunderstorm, birdsong, campfire, a babbling brook, warm strings, harp, kalimba,
  flute, singing bowls, and calming noise and frequency beds.
- Infinite sessions: let a soundscape play for as long as you like, with a guided
  arc first or as pure ambience.
- Instant soundscape previews, and a piece of line art that moves with each sound.

Why relaxed:

- Personal. A fresh script for your intention and your time, not a fixed library
  of recordings.
- Calm by design. A dark, quiet interface with a breathing visual to settle to.
- Yours alone. No account and no sign in. What you type stays on your device.
- Keeps playing. Sessions continue when your screen locks.

Find a few minutes, and let relaxed make something just for you.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes")

relaxed generates a bespoke guided meditation on demand. It writes a unique script
for the user's stated intention and length, voices it with real-time speech
synthesis, and mixes it live over a continuous soundscape, with background playback
that continues when the device is locked. There is no login, open it and start a
session. This update adds a larger seamless-looping sound library (24 soundscapes)
and an infinite session length.

No demo account is needed (there is no sign in).
