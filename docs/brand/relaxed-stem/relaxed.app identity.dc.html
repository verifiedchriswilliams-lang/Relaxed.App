relaxed.app
set in instrument serif, always lowercase, always at optical size. the dot-app carries the only color in the mark — a small breath of accent at the end of the word. no ligature tricks, no icon fused to the type: the calm comes from the space around it (minimum clear space = the x-height on every side).

app icon
the mark is a breath seen from above: concentric rings, soft-edged, warm at the centre and dusk at the rim. no letterform in the tile — at 18px a lowercase "r" disappears, but a glowing centre still reads. the same rings become the player orb, so the icon is literally the product's core visual, not a badge stuck on top.
color system
light-first, warm-neutral. i moved off the placeholder navy + teal: navy/teal reads clinical and screen-lit, and the app is used in daylight on a phone as often as at 11pm. paper-warm light is the resting state; a dim mode takes over for the player and for sleep, so the darkness arrives when the eyes close, not before.

serif tabular · timecode
two families, no exceptions. instrument serif for anything the user reads slowly; hanken grotesk for anything they act on. text-transform is never uppercase anywhere in the product — labels get tracking instead of caps, which is how the lowercase rule survives contact with small ui text.
space, radius, elevation
4pt base · 4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64. mobile gutter 20, desktop 56, content column max 560. section rhythm is 32 inside a card, 24 between cards — generous by default; crowding is the one thing that breaks calm.
radii 10 / 18 / 28 / full. anything the thumb touches is pill or 28 — nothing in the product has a corner sharper than 10.
elevation is haze, not drop shadow: large blur, negative spread, warm-tinted, no ring. flat + hairline for grouping, level 1 for pressed state, level 2 for sheets only.
iconography
1.5px stroke, round caps, 24px grid, built from circles and straight lines only — the same geometry as the mark. icons never appear without a lowercase label except pause and end, where the shape is unambiguous.
motion & easing
breath — 11s loop. 4s in, 1s hold, 5s out, 1s rest. the orb never returns to the same size twice in a row by more than a hair.
easing. breath cubic-bezier(.37,0,.63,1) · enter (.16,1,.3,1) · exit (.4,0,1,1). nothing overshoots, nothing bounces.
durations. tap feedback 120ms · card select 220ms · screen change 480ms crossfade · setup→generating 900ms dissolve.
transitions fade and settle; they do not slide. opacity and a ≤8px lift only — lateral movement implies navigation urgency. audio always leads: the soundscape fades in 1.2s before the player finishes composing itself. under prefers-reduced-motion the orb holds at mid-breath and the caption text ("breathe in / breathe out") cross-fades on the same 11s cadence, so the pacing survives without movement.

the 0.72-lightness accents are decoration and fills only — text and icons use the 0.46 deep pair. every control is ≥48px tall with ≥8px between targets; the pause button is 88px because it gets pressed with eyes shut. focus is a 2px accent-deep ring at 3px offset, never removed. selected states carry a check or a filled dot as well as color. the player announces itself once ("your session is ready, 10 minutes") and then stays quiet for screen readers; the script panel is the accessible transcript.
